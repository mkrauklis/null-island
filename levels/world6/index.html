Area 6 — The Core

The source of the island's corruption sits at the center of this maze: a mutated thing with five tentacles, slowly swinging around it like a fan. Touch one and you're gone. Five scanners are scattered through the maze — reach one and it blows up a tentacle. Clear all five and the door north of the boss opens.
